# Code of Conduct

DutyBell contributors and maintainers must make participation respectful, safe, and constructive.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, and sustained disruption are
not acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

Be specific about technical behavior, assume honest mistakes, protect private data in reports, and
leave room for people to correct errors. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method on their
GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially and promptly as practical. Good-faith
reporters will not face retaliation.
